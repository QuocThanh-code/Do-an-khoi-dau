HUYEN THOAI ANH TRANG - CAC FILE MA NGUON DA TACH

Cau truc:
- index.html       : bo khung giao dien va cac man hinh
- styles.css       : toan bo CSS, mau sac, bo cuc va hieu ung
- game.js          : logic game, chien dau, boss, tai khoan, khoi phuc mat khau, giao dich
- app.py           : may chu Python, API, co so du lieu SQLite, dang ky/dang nhap va giao dich
- requirements.txt : khong can cai them package nao
- run_windows.bat  : chay nhanh tren Windows

CACH CHAY TREN WINDOWS
1. Cai Python 3 neu may chua co.
2. De tat ca cac file trong cung mot thu muc.
3. Nhan dup run_windows.bat.
4. Mo trinh duyet va truy cap http://127.0.0.1:8000
5. Dang ky tai khoan trong game.

CACH CHAY BANG LENH
Mo Terminal/PowerShell tai thu muc nay va chay:
    python app.py
Sau do mo http://127.0.0.1:8000

LUU Y
- Tai khoan, luu game va kho vat pham duoc luu trong SQLite o thu muc moon_legend_rpg_private_data nam canh thu muc du an.
- Khong xoa thu muc du lieu nay neu muon giu tai khoan va tien trinh.
- Giao dich vat pham chi hoat dong giua cac tai khoan cung ket noi toi cung mot may chu.
- Mac dinh may chu chi lang nghe tren may hien tai (127.0.0.1). De cho thiet bi khac ket noi can cau hinh mang va bao mat may chu; khong mo cong Internet truc tiep khi chua co HTTPS va cau hinh bao mat phu hop.
- Cau hoi bao mat chi nen dung cau tra loi ban co the nho. Dung mat khau rieng, khong tai su dung mat khau quan trong.
- File nay la ma nguon de chay thu nghiem tren may ca nhan, chua phai dich vu multiplayer cong khai da duoc trien khai.
